Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse a category, open a product and click Add to cart, then go to your cart and choose Proceed to checkout.

What does pay on delivery mean?

On eligible items you pay the delivery agent when your order arrives, so no card is needed upfront.

How long does delivery take?

Orders in major cities usually arrive in 1 to 3 working days. Other locations can take up to 7 working days.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Most items can be returned within 7 days if they are unused and in their original packaging.

How do I use a promo code?

Reveal a code on the Promo page, then enter it at checkout before you pay.

How do I contact support?
